# Content & AI Use Policy

All content on this website — including text, code, images, designs, and other media — is the intellectual property of Fahd Ashour and is protected by copyright law.

## No AI / Machine Learning Training

Scraping, crawling, or otherwise collecting any content from this site for the purpose of training, fine-tuning, or powering artificial intelligence, machine learning, or large language model systems — whether commercial or research — is **strictly prohibited** without prior written permission.

This applies to all forms of content, including:

- Written articles, blog posts, and devlogs
- Code and code snippets
- Images and gallery artwork
- Site design and layout

This restriction applies regardless of the method used to obtain the content, including but not limited to automated crawlers, scrapers, and manual collection.

## Automated Crawlers

This site's `robots.txt` blocks known AI-training crawlers. This policy supplements those technical measures and applies even if a crawler ignores them.

## Reporting & Contact

If you believe an AI system has been trained on content from this site without permission, or if you wish to request permission to use any content, please open an issue on the [site's GitHub repository](https://github.com/fahdfady/fahddev) or contact me through the platform where you found this content.
