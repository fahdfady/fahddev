I have been maintaining MetaSSR and MetaCall's Rust port ([metacall](https://crates.io/crates/metacall) and [metacall_sys](https://crates.io/crates/metacall-sys) crates) for a while now. [Google Summer of Code 2026](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/programs/2026/projects/nlw0P03Q) has been taking a big part of my schedule and mental presence .And I *feel* got a lot to share, but not that "a lot" of a blog post or an article. Maybe I need something more casual, more frequent. I also need some feedback, I don't want to share a 6-months journey for someone to point out a mistake i could've avoided in 'month 2'

I'm also pretty excited about MetaSSR, and have been writing a blog post about. Procrastinating and perfecting it was a blocker for me. So I figured out that I would love to document my process, without judgment and with frequent feedback.
Also, not being jobless makes contributing to Open Source harder and less enjoyable than a movie night or some qahwa with the bois ([The Coffeshop culture in egypt is something different](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lstnsT2JPnc)). And I believe in open source software and I want to keep doing it. Sharing progress might be a good way to keep excited and invested.

So, accidentally it took a shape of a **devlog**. Maybe not for MetaSSR only. But let's start with what's in our hand. I have really exciting news I would love to share with you and receive feedback.
